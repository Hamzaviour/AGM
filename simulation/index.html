    How it works

    A foodpanda-style rider picks up an order at Cheezious, Johar Town and must hand it over at the University of Central Punjab main gate within 20 minutes. There are three routes:

    • A Khayaban-e-Jinnah Road: 3.00 km, 10.0 min, the shortest. Jams at the Jinnah signal chowk in rush hour.
    • B Canal Bank Road: 3.25 km, 10.4 min. Fast, but its underpass floods after about 30 minutes of rain.
    • C Johar Town inner streets: 4.20 km, 14.4 min. The longest route, but it rarely gets blocked.

    Each agent receives the same percept: the clock, the weather, live traffic from the rider app, and any closures it can see. Underpass flooding is not in the percept. Only agents that keep a model of the world (they remember how long it has rained) can expect it.

    Choose All agents (race) to send all seven riders out at once and compare them. One simulated minute takes 2 seconds at 1× speed. The map is schematic, not to scale.

    Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom (Orbit camera). Keyboard: Space = run, 1/2/3 = test, C = camera.
